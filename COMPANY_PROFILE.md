## Company Overview

**Baci** is an AI-native e-commerce platform that empowers entrepreneurs and small businesses to launch beautiful, fully-functional online stores in under 3 minutes. By leveraging cutting-edge generative AI, Baci transforms a simple business description into a complete e-commerce experience—complete with custom branding, product catalogs, and integrated payment solutions.

## The Problem We Solve

Small businesses in Nigeria face significant barriers to entering e-commerce:

- **Time-Consuming Setup** — Business owners spend 10-15 hours per week on manual e-commerce tasks
- **Complexity** — 80% of small businesses never launch online because existing tools are too complex or expensive
- **Technical Barriers** — Most entrepreneurs lack the technical skills to build and maintain a professional online presence
- **Fragmented Solutions** — Business owners must juggle multiple tools for websites, payments, inventory, and marketing

---

## Our Solution

Baci eliminates these barriers with an AI-powered platform that handles everything:

### 🚀 3-Minute Store Creation
Through a simple 3-question onboarding process, merchants go from zero to a live, professional storefront in under 3 minutes.

### 🎨 AI-Powered Branding
- **Logo Generation** — AI creates professional logos tailored to your business type
- **Color Extraction** — Upload an existing logo and AI extracts your brand colors
- **Template Styling** — Pre-designed templates are instantly branded with your colors

### 🤖 Smart Product Management
- **AI Product Descriptions** — Gemini AI generates compelling, conversion-optimized copy
- **Image Enhancement** — AI removes backgrounds and enhances product photos
- **Inventory Tracking** — Real-time stock management with low-stock alerts

### 💳 Integrated Payments
- **Paystack Integration** — Nigeria's leading payment gateway
- **Multiple Methods** — Cards, Bank Transfer, USSD, QR Codes
- **Buy Now, Pay Later** — CredPal and Credit Direct integrations

### 📱 Mobile-First Experience
- **Native Mobile App** — Full merchant dashboard on iOS and Android
- **Responsive Storefronts** — Beautiful customer experiences on any device
- **Offline Capabilities** — Continue working even without internet

## Technology Stack

Baci is built on modern, scalable technology:

| Layer | Technology |
|:------|:-----------|
| **Mobile App** | React Native + Expo |
| **Web Platform** | Next.js 16 (App Router) |
| **Language** | TypeScript (Strict Mode) |
| **Styling** | Tailwind CSS + shadcn/ui |
| **Backend** | Supabase (PostgreSQL + Auth + Storage) |
| **AI/ML** | Google Gemini (Genkit Framework) |
| **Payments** | Paystack, CredPal, Credit Direct |
