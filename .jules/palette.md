## 2024-05-10 - Mobile Touch Target Minimum Size\n**Learning:** In Expo/React Native apps within the Baci monorepo, `Pressable` components and icon buttons smaller than 44x44 pixels fail WCAG 2.1 AA touch target size requirements. Even when `hitSlop` is used, small integers (e.g., `{8}`) are often insufficient.\n**Action:** Always explicitly define a sufficiently large `hitSlop` object (e.g., `{ top: 12, right: 12, bottom: 12, left: 12 }`) instead of a small integer to ensure the total touchable area is at least 44x44 pixels natively on 18-20px icons.
